FAA Standard Windsock Guide

Standard aviation windsocks consist of 5 alternating orange & white stripes.

  • 3 Knots (~5.5 km/h): Windsock swivels & first segment inflates.
  • 6 Knots: First 2 segments extended.
  • 9 Knots: 3 segments extended.
  • 12 Knots: 4 segments extended.
  • 15+ Knots (28 km/h): Fully extended horizontally.
Note: The sock points DOWNWIND (away from the direction wind is blowing from).
